Anshuman Biswas

Hi! I'm Anshuman. I'm a Ph.D. graduate from the Systems and Computer Engineering department at Carleton University, where I conducted research in cloud middleware performance optimization using machine learning techniques. I was supervised by Dr. Shikharesh Majumdar and Dr. Biswajit Nandy.

Currently, I serve as the Vice President of Engineering at Elastio, where I lead teams building cutting-edge cloud-native data protection and recovery solutions.
